## Scene 2 — The Problem (0:11 – 0:54)
*On screen: Housing shortage, new zones near stations, dense documents, no easy answers. Diagram: Zoning Rules, Transport Changes, Approval Steps, and Timelines all converging on "Where do I even start?"*

> Victoria is facing a housing shortage, and new Activity Centres are rezoning land around train and tram stations to allow more homes. But the information residents need — zoning rules, transport changes, approval steps, timelines — is scattered across dense planning documents. Councils publish PDFs. State guidelines sit on another page. Transport updates live somewhere else entirely. If you're a resident, a council officer, or a student trying to understand how a policy applies to your street, you're left piecing it together yourself. The real question people are left asking is simple: "Where do I even start?"

---

## Scene 5 — How It Works (1:43 – 2:33)
*On screen: Four-step pipeline — Collect, Retrieve, Generate, Respond.*

> So how does it actually work? Under the hood, there are four steps. First, Collect — we gather the source documents: planning guidelines, transport notices, Activity Centre fact sheets. Second, Retrieve — when a question comes in, the system searches across those documents for the passages that are actually relevant, rather than guessing from memory. Third, Generate — a local language model reads those retrieved passages and drafts an answer grounded in them, not in assumptions. And fourth, Respond — the chatbot returns that answer to the user, along with the source it came from, so nothing is a black box. Collect, retrieve, generate, respond — that loop runs every single time.

---

## Scene 6 — Test-Driven Evaluation (2:33 – 3:12)
*On screen: Three test types — Known-Answer, Inferred, Unanswerable — each with an example question.*

> Before we trusted this system, we tested it properly. We built three categories of test questions. Known-Answer questions, clearly stated in our documents — like "What is VicSmart?" Inferred questions, which require combining multiple sources — like "Could my rent rise near a station?" And Unanswerable questions, which test whether the chatbot gives an honest "I don't know" instead of making something up — like "What is my house worth?" This test-driven approach is how we measured accuracy, and just as importantly, how we measured honesty.
