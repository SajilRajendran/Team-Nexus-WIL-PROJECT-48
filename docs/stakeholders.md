# Stakeholders and value

**Owner:** Sheryas Senthilkumar

## Who this helps
| Stakeholder | What they need |
|---|---|
| Residents near activity centres | Plain-English answers about what is changing near their home or station |
| Renters and prospective movers | Quick view of planned housing and development before choosing a suburb |
| International students | Simple explanations without planning jargon |
| Councils and Planning Victoria | Fewer repetitive enquiries; consistent answers that point to official sources |

## Why it matters
- Program information is spread across many long government pages and PDFs.
- General AI chatbots can state planning rules confidently with no source.
- Our chatbot cites the official document for every answer and says "I couldn't find this" when evidence is missing.
- Runs locally with free tools: no licence or per-question cost, and no question leaves the machine.

## Strength of evidence
- Measured: 9/10 correct answers, 3/4 correct citations, 10/10 out-of-scope questions refused (retrieval stage).
- Not yet measured: time saved versus manual search, effect on council enquiries. A small user study is the next step.
